Our Story

Two Decades of Gracious Hospitality

Since 2005, Aurelia Grand Hotel has stood as a landmark of elegance on Mumbai's iconic Marine Drive. We've hosted diplomats, artists, and travelers from over 80 countries, each leaving with a story worth telling.

Our philosophy is simple: anticipate every need, honor every tradition, and never compromise on comfort. That commitment has earned us recognition as one of India's top luxury hotels for eight consecutive years.
