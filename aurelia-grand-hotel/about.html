About Aurelia Grand Hotel

Who We Are

A Legacy of Warm, Refined Hospitality

Aurelia Grand Hotel opened its doors in 2005 with a singular mission: to create a home away from home for every traveler who walks through our lobby. Nestled along Marine Drive, our property combines Art Deco heritage architecture with contemporary luxury.

Over two decades, we've grown from a 60-room boutique property into a 180-room landmark, while never losing the personal touch that guests remember us for. Every staff member is trained to anticipate needs before they're spoken.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

2005

Aurelia Grand Hotel opens with 60 rooms, quickly becoming a favorite among business and leisure travelers on Marine Drive.

2011

Major expansion adds 80 new rooms, a rooftop infinity pool, and our signature Pan-Asian restaurant, Lotus Bay.

2016

Awarded "Best Luxury Hotel — Western India" by the National Hospitality Council for the first of six consecutive years.

2020

Launched the Aurelia Wellness Spa, integrating traditional Ayurvedic therapies with modern spa science.

2024

Completed a full renovation of all suites and opened the Presidential Wing, our most luxurious accommodation category yet.
