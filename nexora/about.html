Our Story

Founded on Curiosity and Craft

Nexora Design Studio started in 2018 with a simple belief: great products come from deeply understanding the people who use them. What began as a two-person studio has grown into a full product design team trusted by startups and enterprises alike.

Today, we partner with founders and product teams across fintech, e-commerce, and SaaS to design experiences that are not just beautiful, but genuinely useful.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you work with?+

We primarily work with fintech, SaaS, e-commerce, and healthtech startups, but our design process adapts to any industry.

How long does a typical project take?+

Most engagements run 4-10 weeks depending on scope, ranging from a single landing page redesign to a full product design system.

Do you also help with development?+

We focus on design, but we hand off developer-ready specs and can collaborate closely with your engineering team.

Can I hire you for a single feature?+

Yes, we offer flexible engagement models, from one-off feature designs to ongoing retainer partnerships.
